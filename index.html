About Inventive Alloy Cast

Pressure Die Casting Expertise Built on Experience

Welcome to Inventive Alloy Cast, serving diverse manufacturing industries with quality casting solutions.

Established in 2019 by merging Acme Alloy Cast, founded in 2011, and Precision Die Casting, founded in 2014. Our engineers and skilled workforce produce aluminum, zinc, and brass pressure die castings with customer satisfaction at the center of every project.
